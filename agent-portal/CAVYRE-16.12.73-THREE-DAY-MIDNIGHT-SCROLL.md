# CAVYRE 16.12.73 — Three-Day Midnight Scroll

The 8 PM cutoff came from the active Calendar engine plus the 780px visual clamp.

This release keeps the approved Calendar design and 3-Day internal scroll, extends the default runway from 6 AM through midnight, preserves earlier events when present, removes the fixed 780px clamp, formats midnight as 12 AM, and keeps the legend after the full schedule. No event CRUD/API/drag-drop/model schedule/CRM/Package/Season/auth logic changed.
