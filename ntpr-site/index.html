What we do

We make creative brands matter.

NTPR International is a boutique fashion, art and marketing firm specializing in strategic communications, audience growth, brand visibility and experiential marketing.

We work at the intersection of creativity and strategy—helping designers, artists and creative brands transform ideas into meaningful visibility and memorable experiences.

  2. 02 Art Strategic marketing and communications for artists, exhibitions and cultural brands.

  4. 04 Experiences Create experiences that people remember—and talk about.
