Maison de VeuxThe app

Maison de Veux, in your pocket.

Your own icon and window on your iPhone and Mac. Alerts when something happens, and everything where you left it. There is nothing to buy and nothing to find in the App Store.

For agents & the team

Agent Portal

Roster, calendar, notes, outreach, tasks and alerts for the whole agency.

Open the portal
  1. Open the portal in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Maison de Veux from the new icon, then go to Settings → Alerts & devices to turn on alerts.
  1. Open the portal in Safari, Chrome or Edge and sign in.
  2. Safari: File → Add to Dock. Chrome or Edge: the install icon at the end of the address bar.
  3. Open it from the Dock, then Settings → Alerts & devices to turn on alerts.
  1. Open the portal in Chrome or Edge and sign in.
  2. Choose Install app from the browser menu (or the install icon in the address bar).
For models

Model Portal

Your schedule, packages, development and messages with the agency.

Open the portal
  1. Open the portal in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Maison de Veux Models from the new icon.
  1. Open the portal in Safari, Chrome or Edge and sign in.
  2. Safari: File → Add to Dock. Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar.
  1. Open the portal in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Choose Install app from the browser menu.

Private by design. Anyone can install it, but it only opens for people the agency has given a login. Installing it never shares anything, and removing it is the same as deleting any app. Updates arrive on their own.
